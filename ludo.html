Ludo

Roll a six to leave the yard. First to bring all four home wins.

At the table

    Seats for the next game

    Seat at least two players to start.

    House rules
    • Roll a 6 to bring a token out onto your start square.
    • A 6, a capture, or getting a token home earns another roll. Three 6s in a row forfeits the turn.
    • Land on an opponent to send it back to its yard. Start squares and stars are safe.
    • You need the exact number to reach the center.
    • Keyboard: Space rolls, Tab moves between tokens you can play, Enter moves one.
